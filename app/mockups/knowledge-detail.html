# Frequently asked questions ## Viewings Viewings are available Monday to Saturday, 10:00–19:00. Each viewing lasts about 30 minutes. Please bring photo ID. Viewings can be rescheduled free of charge up to 4 hours before. ## Deposit and contract The deposit is two months' rent, held for the length of the contract. Standard contracts run for 12 months. Shorter terms are possible for furnished apartments — ask a member of the team. ## Pricing Listed prices exclude utilities and building maintenance fees. We can share the full cost breakdown after the first viewing. Negotiation on price is handled by the sales team, not by the agent. ## What we don't do We don't give legal or tax advice. If the customer asks, explain that a member of the team will get back to them.
