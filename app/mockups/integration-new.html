Connect a CRM

Your customer data stays in your CRM. We only keep a light record of who to call and how to find them again.

1. Choose your CRM

Pick a supported CRM, or connect your own system.

2. Sign in to HubSpot

You’ll be sent to HubSpot to approve access, then brought right back.

This connection will be able to:
  • Read contacts, leads and companies so agents can personalise calls
  • Update records and add notes after each call
  • Create tasks and log activities

Agents can only use the specific actions you allow for each agent. You can disconnect at any time.
