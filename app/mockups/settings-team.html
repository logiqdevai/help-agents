What each role can do

Owner
  • Full access to everything
  • Billing and usage
  • Delete the company account
Admin
  • Manage agents and integrations
  • Manage knowledge
  • Manage team members and settings
Member
  • Use and view the agents and calls they have been given access to
  • Can be limited to specific agents
Viewer
  • Read-only: can look, cannot change anything
Permissions will become more fine-grained over time, so you are not limited to these four roles.
